# How are the documents supposed to look like
- Hierarchical documentation
- Clearly stated inputs and outputs of individual stages of documents
- Documents can be tailored to be useful for multiple standards & processes (safety, security, networking, etc...)
- Documents are used in mulitple argument chains
- Low friction: use google docs/online docs without barrier to collaborate

# What do we need to do in order to get a proof-of-concept
- Clear scope of the PoC
    - What is the minimum viable examples
    - Create something relatable for other industrie
    - Something "graspable" like a level crossing, signal, switch, etc.
- How do we limit the feature creep?
    - Maybe just look at one aspect of safety and limit ourselves to that viewpoint
    - Only look at one function of the object 
        - e.g. signal: just communication interface, making sure only 1 light is on, etc.
