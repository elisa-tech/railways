### Idea of "safety element out of context"
- Railway standards don't know the concept of "having safety elements out of context"
- Every element must be certified for a specific use-case
- Cenelec has a similar concept: "generic products". (Careful not to confuse with generic application)
- EN 50716 may contain rules for using a preexisting product for a new use-case
